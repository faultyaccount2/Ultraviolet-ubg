ReadyPrivacy & policies

Settings

All pages stay inside this app. Proxy is the default. Direct frames can be denied by a site's framing policy; their failures and navigation cannot be reliably inspected. Switch to Proxy manually if needed. Authentication, TLS and rate-limit failures are not bypassed.

The about:blank wrapper and appearance settings only change this app's browser-tab presentation. They do not hide traffic, browser history or activity from network operators.

Colors

Choose a color or enter #RRGGBB. Changes are saved in this browser. Page frame color affects the frame background, not a website's own styling.

Uses the official uBlock Origin core with EasyList, EasyPrivacy and your custom exceptions. Exceptions allow requests to the listed services, including some ads/trackers. Cosmetic filtering and extension features are not included.

Logs only request metadata, never cookies, authorization headers or response bodies. At most 200 records per tab; native DIRECT requests cannot be inspected.

Native privacy permissions, download folders, HTTP cache, third-party cookies/storage and direct-site uBlock filtering are controlled by your browser/extensions, not this page.

Settings and history are saved only in this browser. Proxy cookies persist in a separate per-site browser-local jar; HttpOnly values stay hidden from site scripts. Other site storage and basic CacheStorage remain isolated in memory, never exposing the app's data. Site caches are limited to 200 entries / 64 MB per context and disappear when it closes. Complex login flows and IndexedDB may not work.

Shortcuts: Alt+T new tab, Alt+W close tab, Alt+L address bar, Alt+R reload, Alt+Left/Right navigation.

Site routing

Manual verification uses the whole site in DIRECT, not just its CAPTCHA. The saved route stays DIRECT until you change it. The browser manages the site's cookies and login; they are not copied to Proxy or cleared by Bromine's cache controls. Switching to Proxy may lose verification and login.

CAPTCHA and Cloudflare checks are not guaranteed to work in an iframe. Sites may require a top-level browser tab or block third-party cookies. A loaded page is not proof that verification succeeded. No challenges are automatically solved or bypassed.

Direct remains in an iframe, without rewriting. Sites may refuse embedding. Cross-origin errors cannot be classified reliably; use Proxy explicitly instead of guessing past a TLS, authentication or security failure.

        What works

        This is an HTTP(S) web proxy with an optional authenticated WebSocket relay, not a VPN. It handles public HTTP(S) pages, redirects, links, standard images, styles, fonts, classic scripts and basic GET/POST forms. WebSocket connections are limited to explicitly approved hosts and close at the edge-function time limit.

        It does not use Wisp or a VPS. The optional WSS relay is separately authenticated and limited to explicitly approved exact hostnames. Private/internal addresses and non-standard ports are blocked.

        Authenticated cookies, login flows, relative module imports, complex client routing, IndexedDB, anti-bot checks and origin-sensitive APIs may not work. Binary responses stream without an arbitrary file-size cap; oversized HTML/CSS still has a safe processing budget.

        WebRTC, raw TCP/UDP and system-wide tunneling are not supported. WebSockets require an authenticated Supabase session and an approved host, and no proxy can promise every website will work.
